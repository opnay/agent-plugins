# ASD-STE100 Writing

Improve technical instructions without changing their meaning. Apply transferable clarity principles in the requested language. Use the English reference for English prose.

## Scope

- Draft, rewrite, or review technical instructions, agent instructions, skills, and operating procedures.
- Preserve technical facts, authorization, obligations, conditions, and exact literals.
- Keep principles-based editing separate from formal ASD-STE100 compliance review.
- Do not apply this style to ordinary conversation, literary writing, or marketing by default. Translation, certification, and technical fact validation are outside this skill.

## Workflow

1. Identify the requested artifact, reader, language, register, technical sources, protected text, and scope: draft, rewrite, or review.
2. Distinguish procedures from descriptions. Use principles-based editing unless formal compliance is required; keep that requirement when the user specifies it.
3. Apply the core principles below. For English prose, read [English vocabulary and usage](references/english-vocabulary.md). In mixed-language documents, apply that reference only to English prose.
4. Compare the result with the source. Preserve actions, conditions, order, logical relationships, obligations, exceptions, quantities, units, warnings, authority, and exact literals.
5. Return the requested result. Read [Standard scope and evidence](references/standard-scope.md) for formal compliance requirements, explanations of the standard, or checks of compliance claims. For formal reviews, report the edition, checked scope, evidence, and unresolved checks.

## Core Principles

- Give each sentence one topic or main instruction. Put sequential actions in separate steps without changing their order.
- Name the actor, action, and object when needed to prevent ambiguity. Use concrete verbs. Give direct instructions in procedures and state facts in descriptions.
- Put conditions, prerequisites, and warnings before the action when the reader must know them first.
- Distinguish a cause from its result, alternatives from sequential actions, and prerequisites from optional advice.
- Use the same term for the same object. Replace ambiguous pronouns with the intended noun.
- Keep sentences short enough to understand, while retaining necessary conditions, exceptions, and logical links. Do not impose English word-count rules on other languages.
- State completion, recovery, and stopping criteria when the sources establish them. Do not invent thresholds, replacement criteria, or operational facts to make vague text sound precise.
- When a phrase leaves the decision criterion unclear, use an established criterion or flag the gap. Ask only if the missing information materially affects the result; continue independent edits.
- Preserve the requested language and register. Protect code, commands, paths, APIs, identifiers, UI labels, quotations, and controlled safety wording. Report conflicts with protected wording rather than silently changing it.
- Preserve obligation strength, permission, negation, and logical operators. A shorter instruction must not broaden authority or remove a restriction.

## Result And Review

- Draft or rewrite: return the usable text in the requested format, with a brief unresolved note only when needed.
- Review: report the location, issue, reason, and suggested correction. Do not modify the source under a review-only request.
- Confirm that the reader can identify what to do, to which object, under which conditions, in which order, and how to recognize completion when that criterion is known.
- Principles-based editing is an adaptation of STE guidance, not verified compliance. Non-English documents cannot be described as formally STE-compliant.
- Formal compliance requires the applicable edition's complete writing rules and dictionary, supported project terminology, and review of the applicable requirements. If evidence is unavailable, perform supported improvements and mark compliance as unverified. This skill and checker output do not replace the standard or provide certification.
