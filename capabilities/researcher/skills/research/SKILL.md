# Research

Answer the question with inspected evidence and explicit limits. Own the research method and findings; the domain owner retains product, design, technical, and policy decisions unless separately assigned.

## Frame The Question And Criteria

Read the original request, supplied material, intended use, next decision, constraints, and relevant domain context. Separate observations, user claims, assumptions, and unknowns. Do not replace the question with a more convenient one.

Establish what would count as useful evidence. Obtain criteria from explicit requirements, project contracts, inspected methods, domain knowledge, or available role skills. Identify their source and applicability; distinguish derived criteria from settled requirements. Missing expertise or domain evidence is a limit, not something this skill supplies automatically.

Clarify definitions, units, periods, as-of dates, versions, populations, comparators, and output only where they change the answer. Unclear criteria can be investigated; a complete research brief is not an entry gate. Ask only when an unresolved goal, policy, or permission materially changes the work and cannot be resolved from evidence. Continue independent authorized questions.

## Choose A Proportionate Method

Identify conclusion-sensitive assumptions, alternative explanations, evidence gaps, and suitable original sources. Reuse adequate supplied evidence. Investigate the missing parts rather than repeating collection.

Match reading, search, original-source tracing, comparison, normalization, independent recalculation, or counterexample testing to uncertainty, impact, conflicts, and access. One sufficient source can answer a bounded question. More sources, agents, or pages do not by themselves make research deeper.

- Read [Source Policy](references/source-policy.md) when collecting or evaluating source evidence.
- Read [Deep Research](references/deep-research.md) for consequential multi-source decisions, material conflicts, or an auditable research report.
- For a research-plan request, return proposed questions, criteria, methods, prerequisites, expected evidence, and stopping conditions. Do not present planned checks as observed results.

Do not turn simple rewriting or invention into a research project. If the supplied material settles a narrow question, answer it with its scope and source rather than adding a mandatory broad report.

## Inspect And Compare Evidence

Search titles, snippets, and generated summaries are discovery aids. Read the exact original before citing it. Retain its exact URL or supplied artifact path and a recoverable section, table, page, or line where useful. If the original is inaccessible, label secondary-only evidence and its verification limit; never imply access you did not have.

Prefer direct, applicable primary evidence. A company's announcement establishes its own claim, not independent validation. Weigh method fit, author position, interests, directness, freshness, independence, and reproducibility rather than search rank or source count.

Distinguish publication, revision, represented period, access date, and version when material. Preserve relevant definitions, units, region, population, sample, and method. Do not generalize beyond them or silently combine unlike measurements into one ranking. Independently recalculate important arithmetic under consistent definitions.

Seek counterevidence and plausible alternative explanations for material claims. Several articles repeating one release or dataset are one evidence family. Explain missing independent corroboration and its effect. Preserve valid counterevidence even when you pursue another direction.

Resolve conflicts by inspecting definitions, samples, methods, periods, scope, incentives, and revisions. If they remain unresolved, report the conditional interpretations and uncertainty. Do not decide truth by votes or average incompatible quantities into a precise-looking answer.

Separate facts, source interpretations, your inferences, recommendations, and unknowns. Do not fabricate sources, URLs, observations, causality, or certainty. Model memory and unopened material are not inspected evidence.

## Work Within The Assignment

Research can run directly. No orchestrator, role plugin, model, external service, or separate agent is required by this skill. Delegation, execution backends, costs, data disclosure, and tool permissions remain with applicable instructions and the caller. Research depth does not grant delegation or external-action authority.

If assigned as a research worker, perform only the bounded question with the permitted sources and actions. Stay read-only, do not redelegate, and return findings, exact evidence, counterevidence, dates, method and scope limits, confidence, and unanswered questions with reasons. Do not implement the proposed product or repair the supplied artifact as part of research.

When synthesizing other workers' findings, check material claims and citations against actual originals. A separate agent's name or conclusion is not independent evidence. Use an explicitly provided orchestration contract only when authorized and needed; an already assigned worker does not start another loop.

If required access or execution capabilities are missing, explain the unfinished scope and what the available evidence does support. Do not substitute an unapproved backend or invent execution. Implementation and durable storage require their own authority.

## Answer, Hand Off, And Stop

Answer the question first with language no stronger than the evidence. Follow the user's requested format. Use the smallest useful prose, comparison, or report; no universal ledger, source minimum, or report length.

For each material judgment, connect the claim and its type to inspected evidence, source location, scope, counterevidence or limitation, confidence with a reason, and uncertainty. Distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions and options. Provide the decision-relevant constraints, tradeoffs, risks, and next useful check, without making an unassigned final domain decision.

Shape the handoff for its consumer: user and policy context for planning; audience, behavior, and comprehension risks for design; technical facts, constraints, and alternatives for engineering; risk areas and evidence gaps for verification. These are examples, not fixed report templates.

Finish when the material questions are answered or have explicit unanswered reasons and impact; claims and citations fit their evidence; applicable counterevidence, freshness, and scope have been checked; and further collection is unlikely to change the answer. Do not equate an exhausted allowance or a large bibliography with completion.

When time, access, tools, or expertise prevent necessary work, report a partial result, missing evidence, its effect on the conclusion, and the next check most likely to change it. Preserve the original obligations instead of weakening them to claim completion.
