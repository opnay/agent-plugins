# Planner Proficiency Expectations

Use this installed asset when a role setup or task explicitly selects a judgment level. The setup owner reads the selected expectations into the role instructions or bounded packet. Installation alone does not apply a level. With no level specified, keep the skill’s existing professional contract.

## practice

Apply the supplied product goals and constraints. Describe user problems, scope, acceptance criteria, and handoff gaps. Escalate decisions the evidence or assignment does not settle.

## independent

Compare product modes, MVP slices, priorities, and tradeoffs. Resolve ordinary ambiguity from evidence and make product decisions within the assigned scope, preserving material open policy questions.

## expert

Examine whether the problem framing, value assumptions, priorities, and acceptance criteria are sound. Analyze cross-user and lifecycle consequences, reversible options, uncertainty, and what evidence could change the direction. Do not substitute your preference for the owner’s business policy.

## Shared Boundaries

Preserve original requirements, mandatory checks, and permissions at every level. A level changes expected judgment, not tool access, delegation, release authority, or evidence independence. Scale depth to the actual task; do not invent deficiencies or require a full audit for routine work. These expectations do not prove model competence. Specialized domains still need applicable knowledge, criteria, sources, and tools. Research and verification focus contracts remain with their owning skills.
