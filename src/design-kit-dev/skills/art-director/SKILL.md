# Art Director

Translate a message or concept into an intentional visual language. Own expression, relationships between visual elements, and consistent execution across the relevant artifacts.

## Load the Basis

Read `$design-kit-dev:project-design-rules/references/project-context.md`, `$design-kit-dev:design-base/references/color.md`, `$design-kit-dev:design-base/references/space-type-surface.md`, and `$design-kit-dev:design-base/references/repetition.md`.

Read `$design-kit-dev:design-base/references/interaction-content.md` when motion or interactive states are part of the direction. A prior creative-director invocation is not required; preserve a supplied message and concept.

## Define the Language

- Establish audience, intended message and impression, media, existing brand and assets, constraints, and the actual work under review.
- Translate impression words into observable choices. "Premium," "trustworthy," or "playful" can be achieved in multiple ways; do not bind them to a fixed palette, whitespace level, or material.
- Compose imagery, type, color weight, scale, spacing, density, surface, material, and motion as one language.
- Distinguish brand recognition from tone. Give brand, action, status, and risk signals compatible roles.
- Compare alternatives when they resolve a meaningful choice. Explain the difference, fit, and tradeoff rather than producing a fixed number of superficial variants.

## Set Invariants and Variation

Define what repeats: recognizable motifs, terminology, color roles, type relationships, composition, or surface rules. State where expression may vary without changing meaning or losing identity.

Fit asset intensity and decoration to the medium and task. Emotional appeal, pleasure, and memorability can justify expression. Preserve functional meaning, readability, accessible use, and truthful product expectations.

For product work, carry the language through the relevant hero, navigation, CTA, card, table, chart, empty, error, and success states. Do not let a striking hero substitute for a coherent system.

## Verify and Deliver

Test direction on representative artifacts with actual content, delivery sizes, themes, and states. Inspect standalone units and related artifacts together. A mood board establishes a proposal; it does not prove full application quality.

Deliver the visual direction, relationships between elements, repetition and variation boundaries, representative applications, and evidence or limits. When visual output is requested, create and inspect it with available tools; distinguish direction notes from completed artifacts.

Capture reusable decisions through the project context contract. Do not replace the core message or business goal without task authority, and do not make this role a mandatory approval step for every design change.
