# Deep Research: Evidence And Reporting

Use this contract for consequential multi-source decisions, material conflicts, or auditable reports. It adds evidence depth to the research workflow; it does not require a different execution mode or a fixed agent count.

## Build A Decision-Relevant Evidence Map

Map subquestions, conclusion-sensitive assumptions, definitions, populations, periods, alternative explanations, available originals, and material gaps. Establish the domain's criteria from supplied contracts or inspected methods; record unresolved policy instead of deciding it for its owner. Revise the map when observations reveal a missing condition, preserving the original question and authority.

For each material falsifiable claim, keep enough working evidence to recover:

- claim and type: fact, source interpretation, inference, or recommendation;
- supporting evidence, counterevidence, and alternative explanations;
- original URL or supplied path, authoring body, recoverable location, publication/revision/as-of/access dates and version where applicable;
- region, period, population, sample, definition, units, and method;
- independent evidence families, corroboration status, unavailable checks, and their impact;
- confidence with its reason and remaining uncertainty.

A compact table or equivalent notes can hold these fields. Do not invent missing metadata, public URLs, or independent sources. Treat company self-report as self-report, a forecast as a forecast, and an inaccessible original as unverified. Read source-policy.md for evaluation details.

## Examine Conflicts And Alternatives

Check whether apparently conflicting findings concern the same definition, unit, period, population, or comparison. Explain asymmetries rather than forcing a ranking. Weigh applicable method quality and evidence independence rather than paper, article, or worker count. Separate an observed association from a causal explanation; expose competing explanations and the evidence needed to distinguish them.

Selecting one investigative direction allocates effort; it does not refute other explanations. Preserve verified counterevidence from unselected directions. If corroboration is unavailable, state why and how much the conclusion depends on the source.

## Report What The Evidence Establishes

Follow the requested format. Otherwise answer first, then present the scope/as-of date, material judgments and supporting evidence, conflicts and limits, decision implications, and unresolved questions. A narrow consequential question may need only a short verdict and its evidence; a complex comparison may need a table and explanation. Do not force a full report outline onto every task.

Expose the working-map fields that matter to interpreting each judgment. Link citations near the supported claim, to actual inspected sources rather than search results. Keep the full working map separate unless requested; a bibliography alone does not connect claims to evidence.

For comparisons, normalize definitions, periods, units, and metrics or explain why comparison is conditional. For literature, evaluate design, methods, samples, external validity, and replication rather than paper counts. For due diligence, distinguish confirmed facts, unverified claims, and risk signals; missing public evidence does not prove absence.

Use confidence as a reasoned statement about directness, applicability, methods, independence, freshness, and sensitivity to missing evidence. Do not assign high confidence solely because numbers are precise. State what unavailable data, sensitive assumption, new observation, or source update could change the conclusion.

## Completion Evidence

Before reporting completion, check that each material subquestion is answered or has a reason and impact for being unanswered; important claims have inspected originals or disclosed limitations; counterevidence, conflicts, freshness, and corroboration were considered; citations support the language used; and the answer serves the original decision.

If further collection would only repeat an evidence family, stop and explain any residual limit. If access, time, or expertise prevents a necessary check, return a partial result and the next check with the highest information value. Never weaken the original question to turn an incomplete investigation into a completed one.
