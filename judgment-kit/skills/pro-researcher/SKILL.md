# Pro Researcher

Use this skill to reduce uncertainty before a product, design, engineering, or quality management decision. Research should make the next decision clearer; it should not become the decision itself.

## Frame

Start with the decision the research must support.

- Name the research question in one clear sentence.
- Separate known facts, user claims, assumptions, unknowns, and constraints.
- Prefer focused research that can change the next decision over broad collection.
- If the question is too wide, narrow it by user, context, decision, risk, or source type.

## Evidence

Gather and judge evidence deliberately.

- Check only sources that are relevant to the decision.
- Weigh source quality, recency, relevance, confidence, and contradiction.
- Keep observed evidence separate from interpretation.
- Treat missing or weak evidence as a finding; name what is missing and why it matters.
- When using code, logs, docs, user context, or external sources, state which evidence supports which claim.

## Synthesis

Turn research into decision input.

- Summarize confirmed facts, likely interpretations, open questions, and confidence.
- Identify options, constraints, risks, and tradeoffs that the next role must consider.
- Highlight contradictions instead of smoothing them over.
- Do not make final product, design, engineering, or quality management decisions unless the evidence directly settles the issue.

## Handoff

Prepare findings for the role that owns the next judgment.

- Product handoff: user or market context, problem signals, constraints, opportunity areas, and unresolved assumptions.
- Design handoff: audience, use context, behavior patterns, comprehension risks, accessibility concerns, and evidence gaps.
- Engineering handoff: technical facts, system constraints, dependencies, unknown code paths, and verification leads.
- Quality management handoff: risk areas, missing evidence, state coverage concerns, and release confidence blockers.

## Report

Report the research question, sources checked, confirmed facts, assumptions, uncertainty, recommended next decision, and residual risk.
